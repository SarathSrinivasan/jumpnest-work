Frequently asked

Good to know before you go.

Do I need to book in advance?

Advance booking is recommended for popular sessions because capacity is controlled.

Can I enquire about a birthday by phone?

Yes. You can call support or use the birthday registration form from the website.

Can parents enter the play zones?

Entry rules vary by zone and age. The venue team will explain the current guidance at check-in.
