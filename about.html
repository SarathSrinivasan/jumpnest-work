About JUMPNEST

Designed like a club. Loved like a playground.

JUMPNEST brings together active play, thoughtful interiors and a parent experience that respects your time.

Our point of view

Children need freedom. Parents need clarity.

We believe the best indoor play spaces do both. That means enough room to move, enough structure to feel safe and enough information for parents to make confident choices before arrival.

Our digital experience mirrors the physical space: clear paths, obvious next steps and no unnecessary friction.

What guides us

Four principles behind every corner.

Move with purpose

Every zone offers a different physical challenge so play can be active without being repetitive.

Make safety visible

Guidance is placed where it matters: at the decision point, not hidden in a policy page.

Respect family time

Booking, passes and party enquiries should take minutes, not a chain of messages.

Design for return visits

Children grow into new zones and families can see their history, balance and next visit in one place.

The atmosphere

Warm enough to feel human. Structured enough to feel premium.

The visual language combines deep green, mineral neutrals and warm brass accents to make a children's venue feel considered rather than noisy. The play remains colourful; the brand gives it room to breathe.

The team

People make the space.

Operations, play hosts and customer support work together so the parent experience is as intentional as the physical one.

Play hosts

Visible, approachable team members who help families understand zones and session flow.

Guest care

A parent support layer for bookings, passes, birthdays and account questions.

Operations

Capacity, cleaning, schedules and reporting are managed through a focused admin workspace.

Room for every family

Built around different ages, needs and energy levels.

Every visit can look different. Clear zone guidance helps families choose the right pace, while quieter areas and parent-friendly information make it easier to take a break and reset.

Start at your pace

First visits can begin with gentler activities before children move into higher-energy zones.

Make the plan clear

Session details, age guidance and practical reminders help parents prepare before arrival.

Pause when needed

Families can take a breather, regroup and choose what feels right for the rest of their visit.

A better visit

Small details. Smoother family days.

From the first booking to the final jump, the experience is designed to feel clear, welcoming and easy to navigate for children and grown-ups alike.

Before you arrive

Review session timing, age guidance and pass options ahead of your visit, so there are fewer surprises at check-in.

While you play

Clearly defined zones and approachable play hosts help families find the right activity and understand the space.

After your visit

Simple account tools and visit information make it easier to plan a return, celebrate milestones and stay connected.
