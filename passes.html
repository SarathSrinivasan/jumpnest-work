Questions

Pass details, plainly explained.

Can one pass be used for multiple children?

The product model can support family passes; production rules can define whether a visit is transferable or tied to a child profile.

What happens when my pass expires?

The remaining balance becomes inactive according to the pass policy. The dashboard should surface expiry warnings before the final week.

Can I book a full session with a pass?

No. Full sessions remain unavailable regardless of payment method.
