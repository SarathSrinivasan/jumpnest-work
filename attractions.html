The play map

Six ways to find their favourite corner.

From high-energy bouncing to imaginative soft play, every zone has a clear age guide, a different rhythm and a reason to come back.

Ages 6–12High energy

Airbound Arena

Linked trampoline lanes, rebound walls and challenge markers for children who never seem to run out of ideas.

Safety note: grip socks and zone briefing required.
Ages 3–8Explorer

Cloud Club

Soft climbs, tunnels and slides designed for imaginative play without the pace of the main arena.

Safety note: parent visibility points are built into the layout.
Ages 3–6Gentle

Little Loop

A lower-height play circuit for first visits, younger siblings and children who prefer a slower start.

Safety note: low-rise equipment and defined entry points.
FamilyMixed pace

Discovery Deck

Climb, crawl, balance and reset. A social zone where siblings can play in parallel.

Safety note: clear capacity signage at every access point.
Before you play

A two-minute parent briefing.

Comfortable clothing, grip socks, age guidance and a quick check-in help everyone settle into the space.
